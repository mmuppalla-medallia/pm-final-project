# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic Velocity
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If we do nothing for 12 months, RouteLogic gets displaced from two directions: specialist competitors that solve real-time coordination better than we do, and larger competitors that have figured out the overall experience. As frontline usage keeps falling, the pressure shows up at renewal, and our market share declines.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Fleet coordinators are working around us. They coordinate by phone and text, and they track live fleet status in separate tools or spreadsheets, because RouteLogic is too slow for time-sensitive decisions.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** Frontline dispatchers are abandoning RouteLogic for faster workarounds, and unless we make real-time coordination fast inside the platform, we will lose them, and then our largest accounts, to leaner competitors.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For fleet coordinators, we will make real-time coordination the fastest thing RouteLogic does, with enterprise power still one step away, because our largest account is already trialing a faster competitor.
